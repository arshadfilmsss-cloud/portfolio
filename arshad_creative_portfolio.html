01 / Get to know me

About me.

A little about my creative approach, experience and the way I bring ideas to life.

I'm Mohammed Arshad, a videographer and video editor from Kerala, India. I enjoy creating visual content that feels cinematic, clear and memorable. My work includes short-form content, promotional videos, product visuals and storytelling edits.

I work across shooting and post-production, combining thoughtful framing, precise editing, colour grading, motion and sound design to create a polished final result. I'm always learning, experimenting and looking for new creative challenges.
